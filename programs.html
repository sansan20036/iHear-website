Our Programs

Two ways we build inclusive communication

Direct student support and community education: a global community with local action across Taiwan, China, the US, and Canada.

One-on-One English Tutoring

Free, personalized English communication sessions delivered via Zoom by trained high school volunteers, for students with hearing loss and other communication needs (including ASD and underrepresented ESL learners). AI-assisted lesson plans are built on professional session guides with input from speech-language pathologists.

  • Weekly 40-minute sessions via Zoom (30 min for younger learners)
  • Trained student tutors, co-tutoring model for continuity
  • 100% free, focused on conversation, pronunciation, reading, and writing

Community Outreach & Seminars

Interactive hearing health seminars and workshops for senior centers, assisted living facilities, community groups, and families. Topics cover hearing loss impacts, practical communication strategies, reducing stigma, and fostering inclusive environments.

  • 200+ people reached · 120+ attendees at community seminars (Oct 2025 & Jan 2026)
  • Custom presentations, virtual or in-person
  • In collaboration with CAPA-MC, funded by AAHI (Asian American Health Initiative)
Request a Seminar

Monthly training workshops

Every volunteer completes structured training in inclusive communication strategies, hearing health awareness, and effective teaching. Guest speakers include speech-language pathologists and hearing healthcare professionals.

Mentorship & co-tutoring

New tutors are paired with experienced mentors for co-tutoring sessions: transferring knowledge, building confidence, and keeping every student's sessions consistent.

How It Works

Three simple steps to get started

1

Fill out the form

Complete our short online request form and tell us about your needs and goals, or simply email us.

2

Get matched with a tutor

We pair you with the tutor who best fits your needs (matching typically takes 1–2 weeks) and arrange a first meeting.

3

Start learning

Build a personalized learning plan with your tutor and start your journey toward confident English communication.
